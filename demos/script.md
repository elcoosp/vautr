## Scene 1 — Create your vault

This is Vautr — a password manager you can run yourself, where the server can't read your secrets. Create a vault with one master password.

## Scene 2 — Onboarding

The guided setup walks you through your vault, your first secret, and your recovery options — in under a minute.

## Scene 3 — Add and copy a secret

Add a login, a note, or an API key. Copy it — the secret never touches the server. Your data syncs, but the server only sees locked boxes.

## Scene 4 — Share with your team

For teams, Vautr turns secrets into projects. Create a project, invite members, and share secrets directly to their inbox.

## Scene 5 — Your Emergency Kit

Download your Emergency Kit — a 24-word recovery key that gets you back in even if you forget your master password.

## Scene 6 — Two-factor authentication

Turn on two-factor authentication with any authenticator app. Your vault stays protected even if your password leaks.

## Scene 7 — Activity log

Review the security log to see exactly what happened, when, and from where.

## Scene 8 — Encrypted backups

Export encrypted backups so you always have a copy of your vault that only you can open.

## Scene 9 — You stay in control

Vautr ships as a desktop app, mobile app, browser extension, web app, and CLI. It runs on your own server. Your secrets will thank you.
