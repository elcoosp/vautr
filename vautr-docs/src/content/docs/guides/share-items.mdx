Use this guide to give another person — or a whole group — access to an item in your vault without exposing plaintext to the server. Vautr implements 1:1 sharing and group sharing with a public-key envelope: an X25519 key encapsulation wraps a share key, and XChaCha20-Poly1305 encrypts the payload. The relay stores only wrapped share keys and ciphertext.

## Prerequisites

- A Vautr account with items in your vault
- The recipient's Vautr account (their public key is fetched automatically from `GET /users/{uuid}/public-key`)

## Share an item with one person

In any Vautr client, open the item you want to share and choose the share action. The client:

1. Fetches the recipient's public key.
2. Wraps the item's key material into an encrypted envelope for that key.
3. Uploads the envelope with `POST /shares/`.

The recipient sees the pending share in their inbox and accepts it; the item then appears in their vault. The server relays ciphertext and wrapped keys only — it cannot read what you shared.

## Share with a group

Groups bundle recipients so you share once instead of per person. Create a group with `POST /groups`, add members with `POST /groups/{id}/members`, and attach items with `POST /groups/{id}/items`. Projects can also carry groups: add a group to a project with `POST /projects/{uuid}/groups` and manage its members and per-project permissions from there.

## Work with your inbox

Your inbox lists every share offered to you:

| Operation | Endpoint |
| --- | --- |
| List the inbox | `GET /shares/inbox` |
| Accept a share's payload | `POST /shares/{share_id}/payload` |
| Decline or remove a share | `DELETE /shares/{share_id}` |

Accepting fetches the encrypted payload and unwraps it locally with your keys.

## Revoke access

Revoke a 1:1 share with `DELETE /shares/{share_id}`. For group members, removing a member from the group triggers forward secrecy: their key material is rotated out, so they lose access to future item versions even if they kept old ciphertext.

<Aside type="note">
  Sharing envelopes bind keys to item identity and key generation, so a replayed envelope cannot decrypt a rotated item. The design details live in `docs/architecture/sharing-pki.md` in the repository.
</Aside>
