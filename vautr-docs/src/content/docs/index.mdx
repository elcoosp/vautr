Vautr is a zero-knowledge password and secrets manager. The server validates OPAQUE authentication and version integrity while it stores only wrapped keys and ciphertext — it never sees your master password, your keys, or your plaintext payloads. A binding [Constitution](https://github.com/elcoosp/vautr/blob/main/CONSTITUTION.md) legally locks in AGPL licensing, free self-hosting, data sovereignty, and an exit clause.

## What you can do with Vautr

- **Organize secrets into projects** — personal or shared, with members, roles, per-project permissions, and user groups.
- **Share end-to-end encrypted items** — 1:1 or with groups; the relay stores only wrapped keys and ciphertext.
- **Add second factors** — TOTP, FIDO2/WebAuthn, recovery codes, and organization-wide MFA policy.
- **Work as a machine** — non-human identities with scoped, revocable access tokens.
- **Attach files** — chunked, resumable, encrypted uploads with bounded memory.
- **Sleep well** — encrypted backup archives, a one-click restore test, and a 24-word BIP-39 Emergency Recovery Kit.

<Aside type="tip">
        The server exposes a health liveness probe at <code>GET /health</code> (always <code>200</code>) and a readiness probe at <code>GET /health/ready</code> (database connectivity).
</Aside>
