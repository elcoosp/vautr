### **PREAMBLE**

We, the founders of Vautr, establish this Constitution to codify our irrevocable commitment to the public interest. Vautr exists to protect the digital autonomy and security of individuals. We recognize that software entrusted with human secrets is susceptible to "enshittification"—the coercive erosion of user rights for extractive profit. 

By incorporating as a Public Benefit Corporation (PBC) / Foundation, and by binding ourselves to this Constitution, we legally subordinate maximum shareholder value to the enduring rights of our users. The following Articles are not product roadmaps; they are inviolable covenants. They operate as binding restrictions on the Corporation, its directors, its officers, and any successors in interest.

---

### **ARTICLE I: DEFINITIONS**

1. **Core Software:** The primary server and client applications necessary to operate the Vautr password manager, including all APIs, cryptographic libraries, and administrative dashboards required for full functionality.
2. **Qualifying Users:** Individual users and small teams, defined as organizations with fewer than [e.g., 10] active users or under [e.g., $1,000,000] in annual revenue, whichever is greater.
3. **Standard Formats:** Structured, machine-readable, and unencrypted data formats widely adopted for data portability (e.g., CSV, JSON, or the prevailing industry standard for password vaults such as Bitwarden/1Password export formats).
4. **Enshittification:** The deliberate degradation of user experience, utility, or rights—including the paywalling of previously free features, the injection of unwanted advertising, or the implementation of vendor lock-in—for the primary purpose of rent-seeking or inflating acquisition metrics.

---

### **ARTICLE II: THE SELF-HOSTING GUARANTEE**

Self-hosting is a right, not a feature tier. The autonomy of the user begins with control over the infrastructure.

**2.1 Irrevocable Right to Self-Host:** Qualifying Users shall permanently possess the right to self-host the Core Software for their own use without payment of licensing fees, seat costs, or subscriptions to the Corporation.
**2.2 Feature Parity:** The self-hosted Core Software must remain fully featured. The Corporation is strictly prohibited from reserving essential security, administrative, or usability features exclusively for the Corporation’s hosted SaaS offerings. "Premium" or "Enterprise" differentiations may only apply to infrastructure scaling, managed support, and administrative compliance tooling intended for large organizations, not the core cryptographic or vault management functionalities.
**2.3 No Artificial Friction:** The Corporation shall not intentionally degrade the self-hosting experience, nor shall it require a commercial connection to the Corporation’s servers for the Core Software to function, except for strictly opt-in, anonymized threat-intelligence sharing.

---

### **ARTICLE III: THE AGPL COMMITMENT**

Security and trust require radical transparency. The Corporation shall never hide its mechanics behind proprietary walls.

**3.1 Mandatory Licensing:** All Core Server and Core Client code developed, maintained, or acquired by the Corporation shall be irrevocably published under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0) or any later version.
**3.2 Prohibition on Proprietary Forks:** The Corporation is strictly prohibited from creating, maintaining, or selling "source-available," "Commons Clause," or proprietary enterprise forks of the Core Software. All core cryptographic and vault management code must flow upstream to the AGPL-3.0 repository.
**3.3 Copyleft Enforcement:** The Corporation shall actively enforce the copyleft provisions of the AGPL-3.0 against any entity that attempts to incorporate Vautr’s Core Software into a proprietary, network-service product without complying with the license.

---

### **ARTICLE IV: DATA SOVEREIGNTY**

A user’s vault is their sole property. The Corporation is a temporary custodian, never a warden.

**4.1 Right to Export:** Users must always be able to export the entirety of their vault data—including but not limited to credentials, URIs, notes, and folder structures—in Standard Formats.
**4.2 Prohibition of Vendor Lock-In:** The Corporation shall not employ cryptographic or structural obfuscation designed to prevent migration. While vaults must remain securely encrypted at rest and in transit, the export mechanism must yield data that is functionally readable and importable by competing, standard password managers.
**4.3 No Data Ransom:** The export functionality shall never be gated behind account verification that requires a live internet connection to the Corporation’s servers, nor shall it be withheld due to a user’s account standing, subscription status, or failure to pay. 

---

### **ARTICLE V: THE EXIT CLAUSE**

If the Corporation is ever subsumed by an entity that does not share these values, the community retains the right to survive independently.

**5.1 Condition of Acquisition:** Any merger, acquisition, or transfer of controlling interest of the Corporation is expressly conditioned upon the acquiring entity’s written, binding agreement to honor this Constitution in its entirety.
**5.2 Trigger of Fork:** If an acquiring entity breaches, repudiates, or amends any provision of Articles II through IV, a "Fork Trigger" shall immediately and automatically occur.
**5.3 Automatic License Reversion:** Upon a Fork Trigger, all intellectual property rights in the Core Software—including trademarks, trade names, copyrights, and any subsequently developed patents—shall instantly revert to, or be licensed on a perpetual, royalty-free, irrevocable basis to, an independent community foundation designated by the existing Board of Directors or, failing that, by the Software Freedom Conservancy. 
**5.4 Community Continuity:** The Corporation shall maintain an escrow of the Core Software source code and trademark assets with a mutually agreed third-party custodian to ensure seamless continuation of the project by the community foundation in the event of a Fork Trigger.

---

### **ARTICLE VI: ENFORCEMENT AND AMENDMENT**

**6.1 Fiduciary Duty:** The Directors of the Corporation owe a fiduciary duty to uphold the public benefit purposes articulated in this Constitution. Compliance with this Constitution is a material consideration in evaluating the business judgment of the Directors.
**6.2 Standing:** Any Qualifying User, or any entity acting on behalf of Qualifying Users, shall have standing to bring an action to enforce the provisions of this Constitution against the Corporation.
**6.3 Amendment Restrictions:** Articles II (Self-Hosting), III (AGPL Commitment), IV (Data Sovereignty), and V (Exit Clause) may not be amended, repealed, or materially altered without the affirmative vote of 80% of the Corporation’s Board of Directors **AND** a ratification referendum passed by a supermajority (66%) of the active, open-source contributor base.
